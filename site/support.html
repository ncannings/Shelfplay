Scanning tips

  • Barcode first. The barcode is the most accurate way to find your exact pressing.
  • Covers: fill the square with the sleeve, avoid glare, and hold steady. Covers with a clear artist name or title work best.
  • Wrong album? Pick the right one under “Not this one?” — ShelfPlay remembers it next time.
  • Auto or manual: tap Auto on the scanner to switch between automatic capture and the shutter button.

Apple Music

Allow Apple Music access in ShelfPlay’s Settings. With a subscription, albums play inside ShelfPlay; without one, they open in the Music app.

Spotify

Everyone can open albums in the Spotify app. Playing directly to Spotify speakers and building playlists needs a Spotify connection, which Spotify limits to five invited accounts until ShelfPlay reaches 250,000 monthly users. Help us get there.

Apple Intelligence

ShelfPlay works on iOS 18 and later. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26), the on-device model improves cover recognition. Check Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri.
