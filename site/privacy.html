Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

ShelfPlay is operated by %%OPERATOR_NAME%% (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the ShelfPlay iPhone app and the ShelfPlay web app at this site.

The short version

  • There are no ShelfPlay accounts. We don’t ask for your name or email.
  • In the iPhone app, cover photos are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded.
  • The iPhone app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your record library is stored on your device. It only reaches our servers if you choose to use a family shelf.

iPhone app

WhatWhere it goes
Camera and photosProcessed on your iPhone with Apple’s Vision framework and, where available, Apple Intelligence. Photos are not uploaded to us or to any AI service.
Barcodes and the album text read from a coverSent as search queries to music catalogues: Apple (iTunes Search and Apple Music), MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive and, if you connected it, Spotify. These services receive the query and your IP address, as with any web request.
Your shelf (library)Stored on your iPhone.
Apple MusicIf you allow access, playback is handled by Apple’s MusicKit under Apple’s privacy policy.
Spotify sign-inIf you connect Spotify, the sign-in happens on Spotify’s website and the access tokens are stored in your iPhone’s Keychain. We don’t see your Spotify password.

Web app

  • Your shelf and settings are stored in your browser’s local storage.
  • If you connect Spotify, our server exchanges the sign-in code for tokens with Spotify (this needs our Spotify app secret) and passes the tokens back to your browser. We don’t keep them.
  • If you add your own Google Gemini API key for cover recognition, the key stays in your browser and the cover photo is sent directly from your browser to Google.
  • The web app may use Google Analytics, only if you accept the cookie banner.

Family shelf (optional, both apps)

If you create or join a family shelf, the list of albums on your shelf (titles, artists, artwork links and catalogue IDs) is stored on our servers, hosted by Cloudflare, under your family code. Anyone with the code can read and add to that shelf, so share it only with people you trust. Cover photos are never uploaded. To have a family shelf deleted, contact us with the code.

Legal basis and retention

Family shelf data is processed to provide the feature you asked for, and kept until you ask us to delete it or the service ends. Catalogue lookups are not stored by us.

Children

ShelfPlay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Since we hold no account data, the only data we can act on is a family shelf. Contact us at %%CONTACT%%.
