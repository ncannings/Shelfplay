How it works

Web app vs iPhone app

Both are free. The iPhone app adds on-device cover recognition, auto-capture and built-in Apple Music playback. The web app can recognise covers too if you add your own Google Gemini API key.

FAQ

Questions

Is ShelfPlay free?

Yes. The iPhone app and the web app are free, with no ads and no account to create.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. ShelfPlay runs on iOS 18 and later. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26), cover recognition is smarter because the on-device model interprets the text on the sleeve. Barcode scanning works on every iPhone.

Why can’t everyone connect Spotify?

Spotify only lets apps in development connect five Spotify accounts. Opening the whole Spotify connection to everyone requires an app to reach 250,000 monthly active users. Until then, everyone can open albums in the Spotify app, and Apple Music works fully.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. In the iPhone app, cover photos are processed on your iPhone. Only the artist and album text is used to search music catalogues. See the privacy policy.

Does it work with CDs and cassettes?

It works with anything that has a retail barcode or a recognisable cover — vinyl, CDs and cassettes.

What if it picks the wrong album?

ShelfPlay shows the closest alternatives under “Not this one?” — tap the right one and it remembers your choice.
